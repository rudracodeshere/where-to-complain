Insurance Ombudsman offices: which one covers your state

Last verified: 23 October 2026

For anyone who needs to send an insurance complaint to the right Ombudsman office

You may file with the Insurance Ombudsman office that covers either the insurer's branch you dealt with or your own residence; find yours in the state lookup below. Complain to the insurer in writing first, then file within one year by letter, email or the online portal.

The 30-second answer

  1. Find your state or union territory in the lookup table below, then use the office row in the full list for the address, phone and email.
  2. Four places are split between offices: Haryana (four districts go to Delhi), Maharashtra (Pune, Mumbai or Thane by area), Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow or Noida by district) and Puducherry (Chennai, Hyderabad or Kochi by area).
  3. Under Rule 14, you may file with the office that covers either the insurer's branch you dealt with or your own residence.
  4. Complain to the insurer in writing first. The Ombudsman will not take the complaint otherwise. Then file within one year (details on the claim rejection guide).
  5. You can also file online at the official portal (link below).

Step-by-step: filing with the right office

  1. You: work out which office covers you

    Official contact
    Use the state lookup and the full list on this page, or the official list at https://cioins.co.in/Ombudsman
    Deadline
    No deadline for this step

  2. The insurer: send a written complaint first and keep proof

    Official contact
    Insurer's Grievance Redressal Officer. See the claim rejection guide for how.
    Deadline
    Wait for the reply. If no reply for one month, you may go to the Ombudsman.

  3. Insurance Ombudsman office: complaint by signed letter, email, or the online portal

    Official contact
    Online: https://cioins.co.in/Complaint/Online. Otherwise use the email or postal address in the table below.
    Deadline
    Within 1 year of the insurer's rejection or unsatisfactory reply, or of the end of the one-month wait (Rules, rule 14)

  4. You: track and respond

    Official contact
    https://cioins.co.in/Complaint/Track
    Deadline
    Award is due within 3 months of the Ombudsman receiving all requirements from you (Rules, rule 17)

Common problems

I live in one state and bought the policy in another

Rule 14 lets you file with the Ombudsman whose area covers the insurer's branch or office complained against or your residential address. Either can work; use the one that suits you.

My district is not in the Uttar Pradesh lists

The official page names districts for Lucknow and Noida only in the lists shown above, some with spellings that differ from current names (for example "Allahabad", "Gorkhpur"). We have copied them as published and do not guess. If you cannot find your district, email one of the two offices and ask which one covers you.

The official site seems to disagree with itself

On 23 October 2026 the Council for Insurance Ombudsmen site showed these differences. We followed the contact list that is shown by default and the Rules and FAQ text, and recommend you confirm by email before sending original documents.

  • The Ombudsman page contains a second, older-looking copy of the centre list with different addresses and phone numbers for several offices (for example Bhopal, Chandigarh, Kochi and Kolkata). We used the list that is displayed by default.
  • The home page shows "17 Centers" and says the Ombudsman entertains complaints where the amount is less than ₹30 lakh (the same page also says compensation sought should not exceed ₹50 lakh), while the Ombudsman list shows 18 centres and the Rules and FAQ say ₹50 lakh. We used 18 and ₹50 lakh.
  • Names of Ombudsmen differ between the two lists, so we have not published officer names.

Can I just file online?

Yes. The Rules provide for online submission and tracking, and the CIO FAQ says to use "Register complaint" under "Complaint Online" on its website. For an online complaint the FAQ lists a photograph among the documents.

Quick answers

Which Insurance Ombudsman office should I file with?

Under Rule 14, you may file with the office that covers either the insurer's branch you dealt with or your own residence.

Which states are split between Ombudsman offices?

Haryana (four districts go to Delhi), Maharashtra (Pune, Mumbai or Thane by area), Uttar Pradesh (Lucknow or Noida by district) and Puducherry (Chennai, Hyderabad or Kochi by area).

Do I need to complain to the insurer first?

Yes. Complain to the insurer in writing first; the Ombudsman will not take the complaint otherwise. If there is no reply for one month, you may go to the Ombudsman.

How long does the Ombudsman take to give an award?

The award is due within 3 months of the Ombudsman receiving all requirements from you (Rules, rule 17).
