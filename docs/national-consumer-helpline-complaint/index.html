National Consumer Helpline (NCH 1915): how to complain and what happens next

Last checked: 26 October 2026

For consumers in India with a complaint against a seller, service provider or company that has not been fixed, who want a free pre-litigation route before going to a consumer commission

Call the National Consumer Helpline (NCH) on toll-free 1915 or 1800-11-4000, 8 AM to 8 PM daily except national holidays, or register any time on consumerhelpline.gov.in, WhatsApp or SMS 8800001915, the NCH app or UMANG. You get a docket number. Companies in the NCH Convergence programme are expected to respond within 30 days.

The 30-second answer

  1. Call 1915 or 1800-11-4000 (toll-free) and an agent registers your grievance. The call centre works 8 AM to 8 PM on all seven days except national holidays (NCH contact page).
  2. Or register it yourself on consumerhelpline.gov.in, by WhatsApp or SMS to 8800001915, in the NCH app or in the UMANG app.
  3. Keep the docket number. You need it with your registered mobile number or email to track the grievance and to upload documents later.
  4. Wait for the company's reply. NCH forwards grievances to companies in its Convergence programme in real time, and their responses are expected within 30 days (Convergence Program page).
  5. Not resolved? NCH is a pre-litigation route. You can still go to the consumer commission. Its two-year limit counts from the date the cause of action arose, so do not wait too long.

How do I file a complaint with the National Consumer Helpline?

Call 1915 or 1800-11-4000 and an agent registers it, or sign up once on consumerhelpline.gov.in and lodge it yourself with your documents. You can also use WhatsApp or SMS on 8800001915, the NCH app or the UMANG app. Each grievance gets a docket number, which you use to track it and upload documents.

Filing with NCH: each step and what happens next

  1. Register the grievance

    Official contact
    Call 1915 or 1800-11-4000 and talk to an agent. Or sign up once on the NCH registration form (first name, last name, mobile number, email, password, captcha), then log in and fill in the grievance with your documents. Other channels listed by NCH: SMS or WhatsApp 8800001915, email nch-ca@gov.in, the NCH app and the UMANG app (search for Consumer Affairs or National Consumer Helpline).
    Deadline
    The call centre is open 8 AM to 8 PM, all seven days except national holidays. The Department of Consumer Affairs says a call back facility is available from 8 PM to 8 AM.

  2. Get your docket number

    Official contact
    Every grievance is recorded in NCH's central system with a docket number. On WhatsApp, the Department of Consumer Affairs lists the steps as: give your state, city, sector, grievance category and company name, describe the grievance, upload documents, then receive the docket number (DoCA NCH page).
    Deadline
    The official pages do not give a time limit for registering. The two-year limit for a consumer commission complaint counts from the cause of action, so do not wait.

  3. Track it and add documents

    Official contact
    Use Track Your Grievance and Upload Documents. Both ask for the docket number, your registered mobile number or email ID, and a captcha. The UMANG app also lets you track a grievance and upload documents.
    Deadline
    Not stated on the official pages.

  4. The company responds

    Official contact
    Companies join the NCH Convergence programme voluntarily and free of charge. NCH says more than 2000 companies and organisations have joined. Check the Convergence Partners List for your company.
    Deadline
    For Convergence partners, grievances are forwarded in real time and responses are expected within 30 days. NCH does not publish a time for other companies.

  5. Not satisfied: go to the consumer commission

    Official contact
    NCH says that if the grievance is not redressed to your satisfaction, you can approach the appropriate Consumer Commission. File online on e-Jagriti: see our consumer court complaint on e-Jagriti guide.
    Deadline
    Within two years of the date the cause of action arose (Consumer Protection Act, 2019, section 69).

What is the National Consumer Helpline, and can it settle my complaint?

The National Consumer Helpline (NCH) is the grievance redressal mechanism of the Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India. The Department describes it as a single point of access for consumers across the country at the pre-litigation stage. You can register a grievance in 17 languages on the toll-free number 1915. All grievances go to one central portal called INGRAM, the Integrated Grievance Redressal Mechanism, and each one gets a docket number. Private companies, regulators, ombudsmen and government agencies are brought onto the same platform. Companies that join the Convergence programme do so voluntarily and free of charge, and NCH expects them to respond within 30 days. NCH's own About page calls the portal an alternate dispute redressal mechanism at the pre-litigation level. If you are not satisfied, you can approach the appropriate Consumer Commission. Sources: Department of Consumer Affairs NCH page, NCH Convergence Program, NCH About us.

What can I not complain about on NCH?

The NCH consumer login page lists subjects that cannot be treated as consumer grievances: RTI matters, court related or sub judice matters, grievances against a foreign government, religious matters, and suggestions. NCH's About page says grievances related to consumer issues can be registered. Source: NCH consumer login page.

Common problems and what to do

Your complaint is about an insurer

IRDAI has its own route: the insurer's grievance officer, then Bima Bharosa, then the free Insurance Ombudsman. See our health insurance claim rejected guide and the Insurance Ombudsman offices by state.

Your complaint is about a bank or card

RBI runs its own complaint system with the RBI Ombudsman. See how to file a complaint on the RBI CMS portal.

The company did not reply or you are not satisfied

NCH does not take away your right to file in a consumer commission. Note your docket number and the company's reply, if any, and use them as evidence. Steps, fees and which commission are on our consumer court complaint on e-Jagriti page. Work out your last date with the complaint deadline calculator if it covers your case.

Your complaint is about a government office

NCH handles consumer grievances. For a Central Ministry, Department or State Government office, file on CPGRAMS instead: see how to file, track and appeal a CPGRAMS grievance.

The matter is already in court

NCH lists court related and sub judice matters among the subjects it cannot treat as consumer grievances. Follow the case in that court instead.

What we could not confirm. NCH's pages do not say whether the company must accept NCH's suggestion, and they give no response time for companies outside the Convergence programme. We found no fee on any official NCH page; 1915 and 1800-11-4000 are listed as toll-free. The NCH home page shows the 8 AM to 8 PM hours without the national holiday note; we used the fuller wording from the contact page. We have not listed the 17 languages because no official page we opened names them.

Quick answers

What is the NCH 1915 helpline number and timing?

1915 and 1800-11-4000 are the toll-free numbers of the National Consumer Helpline. The call centre works 8 AM to 8 PM on all seven days except national holidays.

What is the National Consumer Helpline WhatsApp number?

8800001915. The same number takes SMS. On WhatsApp you give your state, city, sector, grievance category and company name, describe the grievance, upload documents, and receive a docket number.

How long does NCH take to resolve a complaint?

For companies in the NCH Convergence programme, grievances are forwarded in real time and responses are expected within 30 days. NCH does not publish a time for other companies.

How do I check my NCH complaint status?

Use Track Your Grievance on consumerhelpline.gov.in with your docket number and your registered mobile number or email ID. The UMANG app also lets you track a grievance.

What if NCH does not solve my complaint?

NCH is a pre-litigation route. You can approach the appropriate Consumer Commission, online on e-Jagriti, within two years of the cause of action.
