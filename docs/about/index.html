About Where to Complain and disclaimer

Where to Complain is a small, free directory that explains, in plain English, the order in which to take an Indian consumer complaint to the next level: the company's own grievance team first, then the regulator, then the ombudsman.

How pages are written

  • Every contact detail, address, timeline and limit is read from an official regulator or government page and linked on the page with the date we checked it.
  • If something is not published officially, or the official page could not be opened when we checked, the page says so and points to the official portal. We do not fill gaps from forums, news articles or third-party copies.
  • Letter templates are our own wording, free to copy. They contain only blanks for you to fill in.
  • We do not publish anyone's personal complaint or story, and we do not collect your data. This site has no forms and no ads. It uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that sets no cookies, only to count page visits.

Disclaimer

This page is general information to help you find the right official channel. It is not legal, financial or insurance advice, and it is not affiliated with any regulator, insurer, bank or company. Rules and contact details change; always confirm on the official source linked below before you act. If your case is complex or the amount is large, talk to a qualified professional.

The site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an agent of IRDAI, the Reserve Bank of India, the Council for Insurance Ombudsmen, or any insurer, bank or other company. We cannot take up your complaint for you, and we never ask for passwords, OTPs, card numbers, KYC documents or money. Official ombudsman services in these guides are free of cost to complainants.
